4. Origami directions

What you are doing: You will write step-by-step directions that tell someone how to fold a piece of paper into an animal. This is origami, the art of paper folding. The point is to practice giving directions in the right order, with clear words. You do not have to be good at origami.
  1. Pick an animal. Choose a simple one, like a fish, a dog face, a cat face, a rabbit, a bird, or a frog.
  2. Think through the folds. If you have a square piece of paper, fold it as you write. If you do not, picture it in your mind. Not sure how to fold it? Keep it simple. Make up 4 to 6 easy folds, such as folding in half or folding a corner to the middle.
  3. Write the steps below. Use one box for each step. Pick a sequence word (First, Next, Then, After that, Finally) and write one action in the box.
  4. Make it clear. Say which corner or edge, which direction, and how far. Start each step with an action word like fold, turn, flip, or unfold.
💡 See an example (a paper fan, not an animal)
  1. First: Lay a sheet of paper flat on your desk with the long side facing you.
  2. Next: Fold the bottom edge up about one inch.
  3. Then: Flip the paper over and fold the edge up one inch again.
  4. After that: Keep flipping and folding the same size until you run out of paper.
  5. Finally: Pinch the middle together and let the sides spread out like a fan.

Notice how each step has one action and a detail (which edge, how far).
